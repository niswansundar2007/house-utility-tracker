Settings & WhatsApp

How to set up Green-API (one-time, about 5 minutes)
  1. Go to console.green-api.com and register with your email, then enter the code sent to your inbox.
  2. Click Create an instance and choose the free Developer plan. Wait about 2 minutes.
  3. Open the instance and click to show the QR code. On your phone, open WhatsApp → Linked devices → Link a device and scan it.
  4. On the instance page, copy apiUrl, idInstance and apiTokenInstance into the boxes above.
  5. Enter the number that should receive reminders, click Save settings, then Send test WhatsApp.

Messages are sent from the WhatsApp you linked. If you send to that same number, they appear in your own "Message yourself" chat, usually with no notification sound. To get a proper alert, link a second WhatsApp number (for example a spare SIM or WhatsApp Business) and send to your main number. The free plan can message up to 3 chats.

How to get your free CallMeBot API key (one-time, 1 minute)
  1. Open the CallMeBot WhatsApp page: callmebot.com/blog/free-api-whatsapp-messages and add the phone number it shows to your phone contacts.
  2. From WhatsApp, send this message to that contact: I allow callmebot to send me messages
  3. You will get a reply with your API key. Paste it above with your number (e.g. +919876543210) and click Save settings.
  4. Press Send test WhatsApp. You should get a message within a few seconds.

Reminders are checked every 30 minutes while the app window is open, and each one is sent once per cylinder or bill.
